# Product Hunt Launch Kit - Video Transcriber

## Product Details

**Product Name:** Video Transcriber

**Tagline (60 chars max):**
```
AI-powered transcription for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram & more
```

**Short Description:**
Transform any video into searchable, editable text with AI-powered transcription. Supports YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter/X. Get instant AI summaries, export to multiple formats, and even generate content like Twitter threads or blog posts from your transcripts.

**Full Description:**
Video Transcriber is a free web app that transcribes videos from any major platform using Groq's lightning-fast Whisper API.

**Key Features:**
- **Multi-platform support** - YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Twitter/X
- **AI-powered transcription** - Fast and accurate using Groq Whisper
- **Smart AI summaries** - Brief Summary, Bullet Points, Key Takeaways, Action Items
- **Multiple export formats** - TXT, SRT, VTT, JSON + Copy to clipboard
- **Write Script feature** - Transform transcripts into Twitter threads, blog posts, YouTube scripts, LinkedIn posts, newsletters
- **Ask AI** - Chat with your transcript content
- **Search & timestamps** - Find any part of your transcript instantly
- **Video metadata** - Thumbnail, duration, word count, language detection
- **Dark/Light mode** - Easy on the eyes
